A place with a long view

Part of the wider Fenland landscape

The surrounding drains connect to the Great Ouse and the Ouse Washes. Local plans for nature recovery recognise the value of creating more wetland habitat here. Our plans for grassland and temporary ponds could contribute to that wider effort.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
