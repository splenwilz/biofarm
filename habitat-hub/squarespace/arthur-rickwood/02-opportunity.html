Making room for nature in the Fens

At Arthur Rickwood Farm in East Cambridgeshire, sheep graze fields set among the drainage ditches of the Fens. Farming and water shape this part of the country, where pockets of wet grassland sit alongside arable fields.

We’re exploring how 15.6 ha could support a greater variety of wildlife, with richer grassland, scrub and ditches. Ponds that fill and dry with the seasons could bring further variety to the fields, making space for nature in a way that suits this place.

If you’re planning a development in the area, the farm could provide a future source of local off-site BNG. Talk to us about what you need.
