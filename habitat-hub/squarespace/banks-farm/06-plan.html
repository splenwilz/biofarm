Plans for nature

What we’re planning

We’re exploring opportunities to turn areas of cropland and modified grassland into a more varied landscape, creating habitats that support wildlife while complementing the wider farm.

Habitat opportunityWhat we’re exploring
Traditional orchardEstablishing orchard habitat to add diversity to the farm
Other neutral grasslandCreating more species-rich grassland
Mixed scrubProviding shelter, food and habitat connections for wildlife
HedgerowsCreating and enhancing hedgerows to connect habitats across the landscape

The final habitat mix and number of biodiversity units will be confirmed as plans progress.
