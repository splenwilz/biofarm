A place with a long view

Where farming, nature and community come together

Banks Farm forms part of Chesterblade Hills, where Ed and his family see themselves as custodians of the land, with a responsibility to care for it while continuing to produce food.

The farm sits within a landscape shaped by centuries of farming, with downland, wooded valleys and the historic Forest of Selwood providing a rich setting for nature recovery.

By restoring grassland, orchards, scrub and hedgerows, Banks Farm will contribute to wider landscape priorities for habitat connectivity, soil health and biodiversity. The ambition is to create a lasting legacy for wildlife, the farm and the local community.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
