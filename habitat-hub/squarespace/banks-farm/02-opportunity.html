The opportunity

Farming with nature at Banks Farm

Set in the Chesterblade Hills near Shepton Mallet, Banks Farm is a planned 27-hectare habitat bank in the historic Forest of Selwood, where the Yeovil Scarplands meet the Mendips.

Working with Ed and the team at Chesterblade Hills, we’re developing plans to create species-rich grassland, traditional orchards, mixed scrub and hedgerows, bringing greater habitat diversity and stronger connections for wildlife across the farm.

The plans support priorities identified in Somerset’s Local Nature Recovery Strategy, bringing new opportunities for nature into a landscape with a long farming heritage.

For Ed and his family, looking after the land means making room for wildlife alongside food production. It’s an approach that puts nature, farming and community at the heart of Banks Farm.

For developers in Somerset, Banks Farm could provide a future source of locally relevant off-site biodiversity net gain (BNG) units.
