Long-term stewardship

Restored for nature. Managed for 30 years.

Registration is only the start. Biofarm takes responsibility for creating, managing, monitoring and reporting on the habitats at Badger Bank Farm.

Funds secured for the long term

Funding for habitat management, monitoring and reporting is ring-fenced for the full 30-year term. This gives you confidence in long term delivery, while protecting the sustained management nature needs to recover and thrive.

From arable land to richer habitat

Former arable fields will be restored as neutral grassland, mixed scrub, trees and species-rich native hedgerows, creating a more varied landscape for wildlife.

Building on what is already there

The new habitats will expand and buffer the ancient woodland beside the farm, while the site’s position close to the River Ure corridor will strengthen connections across the wider landscape.

Monitoring that guides the work

Ecological monitoring will track how the habitats establish and change. The results will guide the work on the ground, allowing management to adapt as nature responds.
