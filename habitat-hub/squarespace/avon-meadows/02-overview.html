The habitat bank

Restoring the River Avon floodplain

Avon Meadows sits alongside the River Avon in Warwickshire, where water, grassland and woodland come together across the floodplain.

Across 17.4 hectares, the habitat bank is creating and improving neutral grassland, mixed scrub, wet woodland, river and stream habitat, and individual rural trees. Together, these habitats will bring more variety to the land and strengthen the connections running through this riverside landscape.

For developers, Avon Meadows Habitat Bank provides registered BNG units in a landscape where the work on the ground is easy to understand: restoring and connecting habitats around the river, then caring for them for the long term.
