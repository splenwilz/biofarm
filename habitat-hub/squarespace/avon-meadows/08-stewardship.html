Long-term stewardship

Restored for nature. Managed for 30 years.

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.

Working with the floodplain

Grassland, scrub, wet woodland, trees and river habitat are being managed as parts of the same landscape, rather than in isolation. Over time, the aim is to create stronger connections across the site and more space for nature alongside the River Avon.

Funding protected for the long term

Funding for habitat management, monitoring and reporting is ring-fenced for the full 30-year term. That gives you confidence that the long-term work is provided for, while protecting the sustained management the habitats need.

Monitoring that guides the work

Ecological monitoring will track how the habitats establish and change. What we learn will guide the work on the ground, so management can respond as the site develops.
