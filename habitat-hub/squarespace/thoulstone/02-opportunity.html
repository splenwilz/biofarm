The opportunity

More space for nature at Thoulstone

Thoulstone Park sits in the Wiltshire countryside near Frome, where open grassland, grazing sheep, young woodland and water are already part of the landscape.

It’s also a place people use and enjoy. Woodland walks, a weekly parkrun and other outdoor activities bring the local community into the park throughout the year.

We’re exploring how around 24.65 ha could give nature a bigger place within that story. Plans could include creating richer grassland, improving the young woodland and enhancing the existing pond, building on the landscape that is already here.

If you’re planning a development in Wiltshire or the surrounding area, Thoulstone Park could provide a future source of local off-site BNG. Talk to us about what you need.
