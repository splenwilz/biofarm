A place with a long view

A landscape people are already part of

Thoulstone Park is more than a piece of land. People come here to walk through the woodland, run, spend time outdoors and enjoy the Wiltshire countryside.

Our plans for the habitat bank would build on that connection with the landscape, improving existing habitats and creating more space for nature alongside the way the park is already used and cared for.

The Wiltshire and Swindon Local Nature Recovery Strategy identifies this area for measures including creating open mixed habitats, restoring woodland and maintaining and enhancing mixed habitats. The opportunities we’re exploring at Thoulstone sit naturally within that wider direction for nature recovery.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
