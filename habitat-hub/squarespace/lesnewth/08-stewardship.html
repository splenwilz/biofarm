Long-term stewardship

Restored for nature. Managed for 30 years.

Registration is only the start. Biofarm takes responsibility for creating, managing, monitoring and reporting on the habitats at Lesnewth.

Funding protected for the long term

Funding for habitat management, monitoring and reporting is ring-fenced for the full 30-year term. This gives you greater confidence in long-term delivery, while protecting the sustained management nature needs to recover and thrive.

Strengthening the Celtic rainforest landscape

Species-rich grassland, woodland, scrub, rural trees and Cornish hedgerows will be restored and enhanced around the River Valency. Together, they will extend and reconnect habitat around one of England’s remaining temperate Celtic rainforests.

Monitoring that guides the work

Ecological monitoring will track how the habitats establish and change. The results will guide the work on the ground, allowing management to adapt as nature responds.
