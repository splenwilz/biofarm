The habitat bank

Restoring habitat around a Celtic rainforest

Lesnewth sits within the River Valency landscape in North Cornwall, where one of England’s remaining temperate Celtic rainforests survives. Across 26.8 hectares, lowland meadow, neutral grassland, mixed scrub, broadleaved woodland, rural trees and traditional Cornish hedgerows will be restored and enhanced.

By restoring and enhancing the landscape around this rare woodland, Lesnewth will strengthen ecological connections through the valley, giving wildlife more room to move and supporting the rainforest’s long-term resilience.
