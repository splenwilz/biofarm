A place with a long view

Part of the Test Valley landscape

Broadlands is part of a much bigger working landscape of farmland, grassland and woodland, with the River Test running through the Estate.

Our plans are an opportunity to make this part of the landscape richer for nature, improving grassland and creating more variety through scrub and trees.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
