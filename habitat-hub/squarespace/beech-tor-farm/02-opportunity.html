The opportunity

Restoring habitats in South Devon’s coastal landscape

Set near Kingston in South Devon, Beech Tor Farm is a planned 20.4-hectare habitat bank on the higher ground between the Erme and Avon estuaries, close to the South Devon coast.

Currently managed as arable farmland, the site offers an opportunity to create a more varied landscape. We’re developing plans to establish grassland, mixed scrub, individual trees and hedgerows, strengthening habitat diversity and connections with neighbouring woodland.

The neighbouring woodland is identified in the Local Nature Recovery Strategy (LNRS) as a priority for nature recovery. By creating new habitats on the adjoining farmland, our work at Beech Tor will strengthen connections with this woodland and contribute directly to the LNRS’s wider nature recovery objectives.

For developers in South Devon, Beech Tor Farm could provide a future source of locally relevant off-site biodiversity net gain (BNG) units.
