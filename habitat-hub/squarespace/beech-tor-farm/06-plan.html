Plans for nature

What we’re planning

We’re exploring opportunities to transform areas of existing cropland into a richer mix of habitats, complementing the surrounding woodland and traditional field boundaries.

Habitat opportunityWhat we’re exploring
Other neutral grasslandCreating more diverse grassland habitat on land currently used for arable farming
Mixed scrubEstablishing scrub to provide food, shelter and habitat connections for wildlife
Individual treesIntroducing trees to increase habitat diversity and complement neighbouring woodland
HedgerowsCreating and enhancing hedgerows to strengthen connections across the landscape

The final habitat mix and number of biodiversity units will be confirmed as plans progress.
