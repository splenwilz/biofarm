A place with a long view

Restoring nature in a landscape shaped by farming

Beech Tor Farm sits within one of South Devon’s most distinctive landscapes, where rolling farmland meets wooded valleys, ancient hedgebanks and the sheltered estuaries of the south coast.

The surrounding countryside is characterised by small fields, traditional Devon hedgebanks and deep lanes, with long views towards Dartmoor and the sea. It’s also part of a landscape where farmland plays an important role in supporting wildlife, including the nationally scarce cirl bunting.

By creating grassland, scrub, trees and hedgerows on existing arable land, Beech Tor will contribute to a more connected landscape, building on the ecological value of neighbouring woodland and the wider countryside.

Care that continues

Biofarm will oversee habitat creation and take responsibility for managing and monitoring Beech Tor Farm for at least 30 years.

With ring-fenced funding and ongoing ecological oversight, we’ll make sure the habitats are cared for and deliver lasting benefits for nature.
