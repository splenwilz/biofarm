The habitat bank

A working farm, restored from the river outwards

Rycote Farm sits within a 300-acre, third generation working farm near Thame. Its 11.6-hectare habitat bank brings four less productive field parcels into a 30-year nature recovery programme, keeping the farm productive while making more room for nature.

Along the River Thame, neutral grassland is being restored to draw down excess soil nutrients and reduce run-off. Over time, this will create the conditions for wildflowers including cuckooflower, cowslip, oxeye daisy and meadow vetchling to return.

Further upslope, mixed scrub is being established with hawthorn, trees, herbs and wildflowers. Rides and glades will create varied edges and sheltered niches, helping the new habitat connect with the wider river corridor.
