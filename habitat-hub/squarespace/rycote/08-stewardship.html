Long-term stewardship

Restored for nature. Managed for 30 years.

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.

Grassland working for the River Thame

Seasonal cutting and removal is designed to reduce accumulated nutrients, help wildflowers establish and limit nutrient movement towards the river.

Scrub with a route to woodland

Hawthorn will shelter seeds carried by crows, jays and magpies, while mapped rides and glades create varied conditions for plants, invertebrates, small mammals and birds.

Monitoring the whole landscape

Ground surveys are supported by drone imagery, giving Biofarm a clearer view of drainage, habitat corridors, ridge and furrow and the river’s course. This helps the team track establishment, respond to change and evidence delivery over time.
