A place with a long view

Protecting a landscape shaped by generations

The Vine is part of a landscape with a long agricultural history, where traditional orchards, pasture and ancient woodland sit alongside the wooded valleys of the Herefordshire Plateau.

For the family who have cared for this land over generations, restoring the orchard is an opportunity to protect something distinctive about the place while creating better conditions for wildlife.

The wider landscape faces pressures from changing agricultural practices and the loss of traditional habitats. By restoring grassland, orchards, trees and hedgerows, The Vine will contribute to efforts to reconnect habitats and preserve the character of this part of Worcestershire.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
