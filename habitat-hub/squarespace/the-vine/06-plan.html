Plans for nature

What we’re planning

We’re developing plans to enhance the farm’s existing grassland and create a more varied landscape, bringing back habitats that have traditionally characterised this part of Worcestershire.

Habitat opportunityWhat we’re exploring
Traditional orchardReinstating traditional orchard habitat, building on the site’s existing orchard
GrasslandCreating and enhancing more species-rich grassland
TreesEstablishing trees to strengthen habitat diversity and connectivity
Mixed scrubCreating food, shelter and nesting opportunities for wildlife
HedgerowsRestoring and enhancing hedgerows to connect habitats
Ditches and watercoursesExploring opportunities for enhancement, subject to survey and design

The final habitat mix and number of biodiversity units will be confirmed as plans progress.
