The opportunity

Bringing new life to a family landscape

Set near Tenbury Wells in Worcestershire, The Vine is a planned 18.8-hectare habitat bank on the edge of the Herefordshire Plateau, overlooking the Teme Valley.

The land has been cared for by the same family for generations, with an existing orchard forming part of its history. Working with the landowners, we’re developing plans to reinstate traditional orchard habitat, restore grassland and introduce a richer mix of trees, scrub and hedgerows.

The restoration reflects the character of this landscape, where orchards, ancient woodland, meadows and hedgerows have long shaped the countryside. It also supports wider nature recovery priorities for reconnecting these habitats.

For developers in Worcestershire, The Vine could provide a future source of locally relevant off-site biodiversity net gain (BNG) units.
