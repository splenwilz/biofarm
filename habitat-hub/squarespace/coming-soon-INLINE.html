What to expect

A habitat bank in the making

Cookridge is an early-stage Biofarm habitat bank near Leeds, within the Nottinghamshire, Derbyshire & Yorkshire Coalfield National Character Area. We’re preparing the site to deliver measurable Biodiversity Net Gain, establishing species-rich grassland and other priority habitats through low-intensity, nature-first management.

Once registered, Cookridge will offer BNG units to developments across the region, secured and managed for at least 30 years. Register your interest now to be first in line as units become available.
