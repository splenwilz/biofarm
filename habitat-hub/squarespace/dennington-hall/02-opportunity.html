The opportunity

A new layer of nature in the Suffolk claylands

Dennington Hall is part of a working arable farm near Framlingham, set within the rolling claylands of East Suffolk. Cropped fields sit alongside woodland in a landscape shaped by generations of farming.

We’re exploring how 22.6 hectares of cropland could support a greater variety of wildlife. Plans include new grassland, mixed scrub and scattered trees, with opportunities for hedgerows to connect habitats across the farm.

The land also lies within the headwater catchment of the River Alde. Creating more permanent habitats here could bring greater variety to the farm and strengthen the woodland setting around it.

If you’re planning a development in the area, Dennington Hall could provide a future source of local off-site BNG. Talk to us about what you need.
