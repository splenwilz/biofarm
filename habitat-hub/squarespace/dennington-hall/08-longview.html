A place with a long view

Strengthening the landscape around the farm

Suffolk’s plans for nature identify much of this land as somewhere that could become particularly important for biodiversity. The surrounding woodland is already recognised for its value. Creating grassland, scrub, trees and hedgerows could help strengthen that wider setting.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
