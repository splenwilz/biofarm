Ecological ambition & long-term stewardship

Managed for at least 30 years

Species-rich grassland

Establishing lowland meadow through conservation grazing, timed hay cuts and green-hay spreading of locally adapted wildflower seed.

Structural diversity

Grazing by ponies and cattle creates variation in sward height and structure, supporting invertebrates, small mammals and reptiles.

Evidenced monitoring

Ongoing botanical and wildlife monitoring tracks progress year on year, from meadow indicator species to barn owls and a juvenile tawny owl.
