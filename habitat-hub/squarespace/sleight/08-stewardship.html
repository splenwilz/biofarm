Long-term stewardship

Stewarding the recovery for 30 years

Biofarm takes responsibility for creating, managing, monitoring and reporting on Sleight Farm’s habitats for 30 years. Dedicated funding supports the full management term, giving developers greater confidence in long-term delivery and nature the consistent stewardship it needs.

Working with the natural seed bank

Conservation grazing, carefully timed hay cuts and locally adapted green hay help characteristic species already present in the landscape to spread.

Grazing that shapes the meadow

Ponies and cattle create different heights and structures across the grassland, making more room for invertebrates, small mammals, and reptiles.

Reading the landscape as it changes

Botanical surveys and wildlife cameras show how Sleight Farm is responding and guide the next work on the ground. Lowland meadow indicators, improving grassland condition, badgers, deer, bullfinches, barn owls and juvenile tawny owls are all part of the emerging picture.
