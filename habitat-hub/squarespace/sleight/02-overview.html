The habitat bank

Our flagship landscape in recovery

Across 70 hectares of former sheep and goat grazing land, Sleight Farm is being restored into species-rich grassland and a wider mosaic of woodland, scrub, hedgerows, trees and ditches.

The work is led by what is already here. Conservation grazing, carefully timed hay cuts and locally adapted green hay are helping the farm’s natural seed bank return, so the landscape recovers with its local character intact.

Surveys are already recording lowland meadow indicators including bird’s-foot trefoil, knapweed, betony and pignut. Areas previously classed as modified grassland are becoming more diverse, with one parcel showing signs of developing into lowland meadow.
