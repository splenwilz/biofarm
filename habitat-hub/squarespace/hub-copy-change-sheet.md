## 9. [NATIVE] FAQ (accordion block)

The v3 copy replaces the old "What is a habitat bank?" section with this FAQ — use the
hub's existing native Accordion block (retitle the section, swap in the 6 Q&As below).
Then paste the updated **`ilinks-block-INLINE.html`** in a Code Block directly under the
accordion — its pills now point at the pillar pages (What is BNG? / The Biodiversity
Metric / Biodiversity units / BNG for developers), per the copy doc's internal-linking
system. ⚠ those URLs assume the new pages are live at their final slugs at launch.

**What is a BNG habitat bank?**
A BNG habitat bank is land that is legally secured and managed to create or enhance
habitats for nature. The biodiversity improvements generate units that developers can
purchase to meet off-site BNG requirements.
Biofarm develops and operates habitat banks, taking responsibility for delivery,
management, monitoring and reporting for at least 30 years.

**What does "coming soon" mean?**
Coming soon sites are locations where Biofarm has an agreement in place with the landowner
and which form part of our habitat bank development pipeline.
If a site is relevant to your development, we can assess what is needed and whether the
anticipated timeline could work for your project.

**Can I enquire about a coming soon habitat bank?**
Yes. Tell us where your development is and what you expect to need. We can assess the
site's suitability and give you a realistic view of timing.

**Can units from a coming soon site be allocated immediately?**
Units cannot be formally allocated until the habitat bank has completed the necessary
legal and registration requirements. Speak to us early and we can assess whether the site
could be progressed in time to support your project.

**Is the closest habitat bank always the most suitable?**
Not necessarily. The right solution will depend on your development location, habitat and
unit requirements, applicable trading rules and current availability. Our team can assess
your complete requirement and identify the most suitable route.

**What if there is no suitable habitat bank shown in my area?**
Tell us where your development is and what units you need. The map shows our current
registered and coming soon sites, but it is not the limit of what we can deliver.
We can explore existing supply, bring together units from different sites and habitat
types under one agreement, or assess whether a pipeline site could be progressed for your
project.
