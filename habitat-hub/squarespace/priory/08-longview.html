A place with a long view

Part of the Stour Valley landscape

The Priory sits within a landscape of farmland, grassland, hedgerows and woodland, with the River Stour and its tributaries adding wetland habitats to the wider area.

The property itself has a long relationship with this landscape. Its established gardens include mature trees, woodland and lakes, with views stretching across the Stour Valley towards Stoke-by-Nayland.

Our plans are an opportunity to make the habitat bank land richer for nature too, improving grassland and creating more variety through scrub and trees.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
