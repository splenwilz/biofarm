The opportunity

Making more room for nature in the Stour Valley

The Priory sits near Stoke-by-Nayland in Suffolk, within the wider Stour Valley landscape. Farmland, grassland, hedgerows and mature woodland all shape this part of the countryside, with the River Stour running through the wider landscape.

We’re exploring how 20.89 hectares could support a richer mix of habitats, improving existing grassland and creating areas of scrub and trees.

It’s an opportunity to bring more variety to the land and create more space for nature within an already well-connected rural landscape.

If you’re planning a development in the area, The Priory could provide a source of local off-site BNG. Talk to us about what you need.
