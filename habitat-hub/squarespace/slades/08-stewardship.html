Long-term stewardship

Restored for nature

Wildflower-rich grassland

Other neutral grassland will be created and enhanced, bringing greater variety and more food and shelter for wildlife.

A priority woodland habitat

Lowland mixed deciduous woodland will be created and enhanced alongside scrub, trees and retained hedgerows, strengthening the wooded character of the Greensand Hills.

Monitoring that guides the work

Ecological monitoring will track how the habitats establish and change. The results will guide the work on the ground, allowing management to adapt as nature responds. Acoustic monitoring indicated the presence of 52 bird species during dawn and dusk surveys in May 2026. The findings give us a useful baseline and will help us track how bird life responds as the habitat establishes and matures.
