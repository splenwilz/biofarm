The habitat bank

A richer, more connected landscape

Slades Farm is near Bramley, south of Guildford, within the Wealden Greensand landscape. Across 88 hectares, wildflower-rich grassland, mixed scrub, trees and lowland mixed deciduous woodland will be created and enhanced.

Lowland mixed deciduous woodland is a nationally recognised priority habitat. Together with the grassland, scrub and retained hedgerows, it will provide more food, shelter and nesting opportunities for birds, bats and invertebrates, while helping wildlife move through the wider countryside.
