A place with a long view

Part of the Cotswold landscape

Highfold Farm sits within a wider landscape of grassland, hedgerows and woodland. Nearby commons are known for their limestone grassland, wildflowers and orchids, while woodland and hedgerows provide important connections for wildlife.

Some of that character can already be seen at Highfold, where orchids, salad burnet and small scabious have been recorded. Our plans will look at how the land can build on what is already here, while fitting naturally into the wider landscape.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
