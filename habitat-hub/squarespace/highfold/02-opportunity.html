The opportunity

Making more room for nature in the Cotswolds

Highfold Farm sits near Painswick, in a Cotswold landscape of grassland, hedgerows and woodland.

The land is currently a mix of arable fields and grassland cut for hay. There are already interesting plants here too, including orchids, salad burnet and small scabious.

We’re exploring how 18.4 hectares could support a greater variety of wildlife, with richer grassland alongside scrub, trees and hedgerows. The exact mix will be confirmed as the plans develop.

If you’re planning a development in the area, Highfold Farm could provide a source of local off-site BNG. Talk to us about what you need.
