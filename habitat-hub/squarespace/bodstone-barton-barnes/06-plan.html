Plans for nature

What we’re planning

We’re exploring opportunities to enhance land currently managed for hay production and grazing, creating a richer mix of habitats around the existing ancient woodland valley.

Habitat opportunityWhat we’re exploring
Lowland meadowRestoring more species-rich grassland to support a wider range of plants and wildlife
Mixed scrubCreating shelter, food sources and habitat connections around the woodland
TreesEstablishing additional tree cover to strengthen the surrounding habitat network

The final habitat mix and number of biodiversity units will be confirmed as plans progress.
