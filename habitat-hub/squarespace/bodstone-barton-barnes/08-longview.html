A place with a long view

Protecting nature for the next generation

The landscape around Bodstone Barton Barnes is shaped by rolling pasture, ancient woodland and the steep wooded valleys that characterise this part of North Devon.

For the landowners, protecting this landscape is a personal commitment. Their passion for nature has already inspired them to publish children’s books that help young people understand the importance of nature restoration.

That same commitment is shaping the plans for Bodstone Barton Barnes. By restoring meadow habitat and creating new scrub and tree cover around the ancient woodland valley, the project aims to strengthen the connections between habitats and support wildlife over the long term.

It’s an opportunity to protect what makes this landscape special while helping the next generation understand its value.

Care that continues
