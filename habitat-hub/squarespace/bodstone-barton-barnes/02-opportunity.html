The opportunity

Bringing nature back to North Devon’s coastal landscape

Set near Ilfracombe, Bodstone Barton Barnes is a planned 11-hectare habitat bank in the Exmoor National Character Area, surrounded by the distinctive hills and wooded valleys of North Devon.

The land surrounds a valley of ancient woodland, creating an opportunity to strengthen the habitats around it. Working with the landowners, we’re developing plans to restore lowland meadow and introduce mixed scrub and trees, creating a more varied landscape for wildlife.

The site is identified in the Local Nature Recovery Strategy (LNRS) for grassland and woody habitat creation. Our plans will build on this opportunity, restoring meadow habitat and strengthening connections with the surrounding ancient woodland.

For developers in North Devon, Bodstone Barton Barnes could provide a future source of locally relevant off-site biodiversity net gain (BNG) units.
