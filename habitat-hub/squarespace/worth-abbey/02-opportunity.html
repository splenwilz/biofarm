The opportunity

Recreating Worth Abbey’s lost parkland

Worth Abbey is a working Benedictine monastery, school and community with a long tradition of stewardship. Our partnership builds on that commitment, with the land considered as part of the Abbey’s wider future rather than as an isolated habitat project.

Historic mapping shows that this land once formed part of Paddockhurst Park, before much of its wood pasture and parkland gave way to arable use. Some original trees remain, tracing the old parish boundary.

Biofarm is exploring how 24.6 hectares of sheep-grazed and hay-cut grassland could become a more varied High Weald landscape. The emerging design considers lowland meadow, enhanced neutral grassland, trees returned to their historic positions and hedgerow opportunities.

The habitat mix and unit output will be confirmed as the site progresses. Talk to us about your requirement and we can explore whether Worth Abbey could offer the right fit for your development.
