Recreating Worth Abbey’s lost parkland

Worth Abbey is a working Benedictine monastery, school and community, set within the High Weald. Caring for this land has long been part of life here.

Historic mapping shows that this land once formed part of Paddockhurst Park, with wood pasture and parkland where there are now areas of grassland. Some of the original trees still remain, tracing the old parish boundary.

We’re exploring how 24.6 ha could become a richer, more varied landscape again. Plans include creating lowland meadows, improving existing grassland, bringing trees back into their historic positions, and exploring opportunities for new hedgerows.
