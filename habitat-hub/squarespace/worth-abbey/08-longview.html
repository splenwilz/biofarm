A place with a long view

Rooted in the Abbey landscape

Part of a landscape bigger than the site

Worth Abbey sits within Weald to Waves, a nature recovery corridor connecting habitats from the High Weald towards the Sussex coast. The area has also been identified through the West Sussex Local Nature Recovery Strategy as having potential to become more important for biodiversity.

By restoring meadow, grassland, trees and hedgerows here, the plans for Worth Abbey have the potential to strengthen nature beyond the boundaries of the habitat bank itself.

Care that continues

We’ll create and care for the habitats over the long term, checking how they develop and reporting on progress. You’ll work directly with us throughout, with a team you can turn to as your project moves forward.
