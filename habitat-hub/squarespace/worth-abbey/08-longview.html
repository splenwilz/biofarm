A place with a long view

Rooted in the Abbey community

Part of a landscape bigger than the site

Worth Abbey forms part of Weald to Waves, a nature recovery corridor connecting habitats from the High Weald towards the Sussex coast. The site also sits within an area identified by the West Sussex Local Nature Recovery Strategy as one that could become important for biodiversity. These connections can guide the emerging design, while project-specific metric and trading checks will still determine suitability for any development.

Stewardship that continues after registration

Biofarm will take responsibility for habitat delivery, management, monitoring, and reporting for the legally secured term. Funding will be set aside for that work, giving developers confidence that delivery is planned for the long term and nature the consistent care it needs.
