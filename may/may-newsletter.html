Delivering a local BNG solution that kept Onyx moving

Early collaboration between Biofarm and Onyx helped unlock a locally aligned BNG solution for a commercial scheme in Cornwall.

With no suitable local units available, we brought forward the registration of our Lesnewth habitat bank, enabling the project to progress while supporting long-term habitat restoration in the region.

How drone technology is improving habitat delivery

At Rycote Farm in South Oxfordshire, Biofarm's new drone technology is helping us deliver BNG with greater accuracy and insight.

From mapping habitat corridors to monitoring river systems, aerial surveying gives our ecology team a more detailed understanding of how sites function, supporting smarter decisions and stronger outcomes for developers, landowners and nature.

Stacking BNG: how Biofarm is leading the next phase of nature returns

Most BNG schemes focus on what the land needs to deliver today. We focus on what it can deliver next.

Through stacking, we design habitats that evolve over time, creating long-term ecological value, stronger natural capital outcomes and future opportunities for landowners and developers alike.

This is about moving beyond short-term compliance and thinking more strategically about how landscapes mature, recover and perform over decades.
