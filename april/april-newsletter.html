Government response confirms it: BNG is settling. Off-site is now central

 

The latest government response to BNG consultations does something important. It brings clarity.

Not just on direction, but on how delivery will work in practice.

The 10% requirement remains. But delivery is becoming more practical, more flexible, and easier to plan for.

Key changes are being implemented through 2026. This includes updates to off-site delivery ahead of July 2026, and BNG applying to Nationally Significant Infrastructure Projects (NSIPs) from November 2026.

Avon Fire and Rescue Service: when space is tight, progress should not be

 

Public services do not have spare land. Fire stations, hospitals and emergency hubs are built to serve people first. Every square metre has a purpose.

And these sites need to keep evolving. Expanding, reconfiguring, responding to demand.

BNG needs to work within that reality. Delivering it on site can mean setting land aside. Space that may be needed in the future.

It doesn’t have to work that way.

Worth Abbey arrives: a landmark habitat bank for the High Weald

 

A major new habitat bank is coming to market in Mid Sussex. Built for developers who need BNG that holds up in policy and in practice.

Biodiversity Net Gain: a Landowner Perspective from Christy Wells, Natural Capital Specialist, Fisher German

 

As the BNG market continues to evolve, the focus for many landowners is shifting from whether to participate to how BNG fits within the long-term structure of their land management and rural businesses.

Historically, environmental schemes have compensated landowners based on “income foregone and cost incurred.” BNG, by contrast, introduces a market mechanism where biodiversity improvements can generate long-term commercial value in their own right.
