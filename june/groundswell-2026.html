Panel discussion

BNG on a Working Farm: Opportunity, Reality and Long-Term Commitment

Biodiversity Net Gain is attracting growing interest from farmers and landowners. But where does it fit within a working farm business?

Join us for an open discussion exploring the opportunities, challenges and realities of BNG in practice, featuring leading voices from farming, policy, nature recovery and land management, including:

• Martin Lines, Nature Friendly Farming Network
• Stephen Panks, Natural England
• Vicki Hird, The Wildlife Trusts
• Toby Diggens, Digg & Co.
• Ed Green, Farmer and Landowner
• Edward Hutley, Landowner
• Simon Nash, Biofarm

Hosted by Chris Rowland-Smith, Land Director at Biofarm.

Wednesday 1 July  |  12.00pm – 12.45pm

Thursday 2 July  |  12.00pm – 12.45pm
