Subject Line: BNG in action at Lesnewth | Invitation & agenda | 24 November

We're looking forward to welcoming you to Lesnewth Habitat Bank for a morning exploring how biodiversity net gain is taking shape in Cornwall.

Together with our landowners, Simon and Natasha, we'll share what's happening at Lesnewth and explore how locally delivered BNG can help developers meet their planning requirements while supporting Cornwall's nature recovery priorities.

It's a chance to get out on the land, exchange ideas and see the benefits of working together.

Event details

Date: Tuesday 24 November 2026
Time: 10.30am–1.00pm, with optional lunch afterwards
Location: Old Rectory, Lesnewth, Boscastle, PL35 0HR

The morning's programme

10.30–11.00am | Welcome and introductions
Coffee, meet the Biofarm team and our landowners, Simon and Natasha, and hear about our plans for Lesnewth.

11.00am–1.00pm | BNG in action
An interactive walk and discussion exploring the restoration underway, opportunities for local BNG delivery and the wider benefits for Cornwall.

From 1.00pm | Lunch and conversation
For those who can stay, we'd love you to join us for lunch and a chance to continue the conversation.

Please RSVP to confirm your attendance and, if staying for lunch, any dietary requirements.

We look forward to seeing you there.

The Biofarm team
