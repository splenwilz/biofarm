Richard Graham is partnering with Biofarm to create a thriving habitat bank at Badger Bank Farm, supporting local development and a lasting future for nature.

Local by nature | Badger Bank Farm, North Yorkshire

At Badger Bank Farm, we’re transforming 30 hectares of predominantly arable land into a thriving mix of grassland, scrub, trees and hedgerow. With 232 BNG units across the scheme, this registered habitat bank brings together a practical solution for developers and a lasting investment in North Yorkshire’s landscape.

Set beside ancient woodland and close to the River Ure corridor, Badger Bank will help nature thrive beyond its boundaries. Species-rich grassland, native scrub and scattered trees will create more space for farmland birds, provide feeding routes for bats and help reduce nutrient runoff into the river catchment.

For developers, that means access to grassland, scrub, individual tree and hedgerow units, backed by Biofarm’s long-term management and monitoring. Funding is ring-fenced for the full 33-year term, supporting the work needed to establish these habitats and care for them as they grow.

Richard Graham, landowner at Badger Bank, said:

“Working with Biofarm gives us the expertise and long-term support to make these plans a reality. We want to create more space for wildlife, connect the woodland around us and improve how the land holds and filters water. It’s about leaving the land in better shape for the future.”

BNG fact file: what changed and what stayed

BNG rules changed over the summer. The system is now more proportionate, but the 10% requirement and market for off-site units remain firmly in place.

THE 10% REQUIREMENT STILL STANDS

  • Qualifying developments in England must deliver at least 10% BNG.
  • The main exemptions include home extensions and loft conversions, work permitted without a full planning application, sites of 0.2 hectares or less and temporary developments of five years or less.
  • Priority habitat protections still apply.
  • Self-build and custom-build developments are no longer automatically exempt.

OFF-SITE BNG IS MORE ACCESSIBLE

  • On-site habitat creation, on-site enhancement and registered off-site gains now have equal priority.
  • This applies to non-major developments (fewer than 10 homes or smaller commercial schemes below the major development thresholds).
  • Developers can choose the most practical route from the outset.
  • This supports continued demand for high-integrity units and long-term opportunities for landowners.
  • Statutory credits remain the last resort.

MAJOR INFRASTRUCTURE IS NEXT

  • From 2 November 2026, BNG will become mandatory for Nationally Significant Infrastructure Project applications.
  • This includes major energy, transport and water schemes.
