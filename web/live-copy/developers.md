## When is off-site Biodiversity Net Gain the right solution?
Every development is different, and the most appropriate Biodiversity Net Gain strategy will depend on the type of development, site characteristics and the current regulatory framework.
For many developments, professionally managed off-site biodiversity units provide a practical, efficient and commercially attractive solution.
Biofarm works with developers to identify the most appropriate approach and, where off-site delivery is the right option, provides biodiversity units through our own professionally managed habitat banks.

## Frequently asked by developers

When should I engage Biofarm?
The earlier the better.
Engaging Biofarm during site appraisal or the early planning stages provides greater flexibility and helps avoid unnecessary delays.
Can Biofarm work alongside our planning consultant or ecologist?
Yes.
We regularly work as part of a wider project team, complementing your planning consultants and ecologists with specialist off site Biodiversity Net Gain expertise.
What are biodiversity units?
Biodiversity units measure the biodiversity value created through habitat enhancement and are assessed using the statutory biodiversity metric.
Biofarm creates and manages habitat banks that generate biodiversity units for qualifying developments.
How long are habitat banks managed?
Every Biofarm habitat bank is professionally managed, monitored and reported on for a minimum of 30 years.
Why choose off-site Biodiversity Net Gain?
Off site Biodiversity Net Gain enables developers to secure biodiversity units where on site delivery is not possible or not the preferred solution.
How much does off-site Biodiversity Net Gain cost?
Every project is different.
We believe in fair, transparent pricing and provide clear guidance from the outset.
