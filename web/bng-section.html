New BNG rules: off-site first for minor sites

Go straight to off-site, from day one.

The biodiversity gain hierarchy has changed. For planning applications made from 6 August 2026, minor sites above 0.2 hectares no longer need to exhaust on-site options first — off-site biodiversity gains now carry the same weight as on-site, from the first instance.

Keep your masterplan for what it’s actually for, and secure your biodiversity units from one of our habitat banks from the outset.

The changes at a glance

  • Minor sites above 0.2 hectares can now go straight to off-site biodiversity units — no need to exhaust on-site options first.
  • Sites of 0.2 hectares or below are exempt from mandatory BNG, unless priority habitat is affected.
  • Temporary development of five years or less is exempt.
  • Self-build and custom-build schemes are no longer automatically exempt — many now fall under the small-sites exemption instead.

Applies to planning applications made on or after 6 August 2026. Not sure where your scheme lands? We’ll help you work it out.
