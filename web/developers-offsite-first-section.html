New BNG rules · from 6 August 2026

Go straight to off-site, from day one

The biodiversity gain hierarchy has changed. For planning applications made from 6 August 2026, minor sites above 0.2 hectares no longer need to exhaust on-site options first — off-site biodiversity gains now carry the same weight as on-site, from the first instance.

In practice, that means you can design your site for what it’s actually for — and secure your biodiversity units from one of our habitat banks from the outset.

Keep your masterplan

No more carving habitat out of a tight site. Use your land for development, and let an established habitat bank deliver the biodiversity.

Certainty at planning

Units from legally secured habitat banks, managed and monitored for at least 30 years — a clear, robust line in your biodiversity gain plan.

Sorted early

Bring us in at pre-app. We’ll match your scheme to locally relevant units and stay with you through approval, compliance and beyond.

Also new: sites of 0.2 hectares or below are now exempt from mandatory BNG unless priority habitat is affected, and temporary development of five years or less is exempt too. Not sure where your scheme lands under the new rules? We’ll help you work it out.
