Sessions are designed for developers, promoters, house builders, planners, architects, landscape architects and other professionals involved in development.

  1. 01

    BNG and the planning process

    Understand where BNG fits and what your team needs to know at different stages of a project.

  2. 02

    Get to grips with what drives a project’s BNG requirement and the factors that can influence the outcome.

  3. 03

    On-site and off-site BNG

    Understand the options available and how to weigh them up against the needs of the project.

  4. 04

    Viability and delivery

    Explore where BNG can affect cost, design and delivery, and the choices available to your team.

  5. 05

    The off-site market

    Understand how biodiversity units work, what to look for in a provider and how the market is evolving.

  6. 06

    Your questions and projects
