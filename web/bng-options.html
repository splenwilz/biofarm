New BNG rules · from 6 August 2026

Go straight to off-site, from day one

The biodiversity gain hierarchy has changed. For planning applications made from 6 August 2026, minor sites above 0.2 hectares no longer need to exhaust on-site options first — off-site gains now carry the same weight as on-site.

Keep your masterplan for what it’s actually for, and secure your biodiversity units from one of our habitat banks from the outset.

Off-site, from the outset

Minor sites above 0.2 hectares can go straight to off-site biodiversity units.

0.2ha or below

The smallest sites are now exempt, unless priority habitat is affected.

Five years or less

Temporary development is now exempt from mandatory BNG.

Self-build

No longer automatically exempt — many schemes now fall under the small-sites exemption.
