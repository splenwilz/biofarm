BNG on a Working Farm: Opportunity, Reality & Long-Term Commitment

Biodiversity Net Gain is attracting growing interest from farmers and landowners. But where does it fit within a working farm business?

12.00pm – 12.45pm
Thursday 2 July

Join the conversation

Join Biofarm and a panel of leading voices from farming, policy, nature recovery and land management for an open discussion on the realities of BNG in practice.

The Panel

Hear from leading voices across farming, land management and nature recovery

Hosted by Chris Rowland-Smith, Land Manager at Biofarm. Across the two days, panel members include:

Martin Lines

CEO, Nature Friendly Farming Network

Farmer, industry leader and one of the UK’s most influential voices on the future of sustainable farming.

Stephen Panks

Senior Specialist, Biodiversity Net Gain, Natural England

Bringing insight into the policy, delivery and practical realities of BNG.

Vicki Hird

Strategic Lead – Agriculture, The Wildlife Trusts

Author, campaigner and leading advocate for nature-friendly farming and land management.

Toby Diggens

Founder & Co-Director, Digg & Co.

Landscape restoration specialist with extensive experience creating and restoring species-rich habitats.

Edward Hurley

Landowner

Sharing first-hand experience of exploring and adopting BNG on his own land, including the decisions, opportunities and challenges encountered along the way.

Ed Green

Farmer and Landowner

Offering a practical farming perspective on managing land in a changing rural economy.

Simon Nash

Head of Habitat Management, Biofarm

Habitat restoration specialist with extensive experience delivering and managing biodiversity projects across the UK.

On the agenda

What we’ll discuss

  • Where BNG fits within a working farm business
  • The opportunities and limitations of BNG
  • Lessons from landowners already involved in projects
  • How BNG compares to other diversification options
  • Long-term management, stewardship and commitment
  • Your questions, answered live by the panel
