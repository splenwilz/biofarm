# Infrastructure BNG page: imagery shortlist (first pass, 13 Oct 2026)

Brief from Vix: the page needs imagery of large-scale infrastructure so the reader sees we understand the scale of NSIP programmes, not just habitat banks.

This is a first pass from an open source (Wikimedia Commons, which carries the Geograph UK archive). Unsplash and Pexels need API keys and were not searched. Every image here is Creative Commons: free to use, including commercially, with a credit line (photographer, licence). CC BY-SA also requires that any edited version is shared under the same licence; using the photo as-is with a credit is fine.

Treat this as the **brief for what to buy** as much as a pick list. If the same subjects are bought from Alamy or Adobe Stock, the credit lines go away and the quality goes up.

Preview copies (1600 px, for layout only) are in `web/assets/stock-preview/`. Two are dropped into the "Built around your project" section of `web/infrastructure-bng.html` so the idea can be judged in place.

## What to buy, if buying

Subjects that worked on the sheet and are worth searching on Alamy or Adobe Stock, where the quality and light will be better:

- A new viaduct or road bridge in a green valley, under construction or just finished.
- An aerial of a road or rail corridor cutting through farmland, earthworks still visible.
- A transmission line or substation in open countryside, soft light.
- A solar or wind farm with hedgerows and trees in frame, not just panels or turbines.
- A reservoir, flood scheme or pumping station in a rural setting.

Avoid: hard blue skies, hi-vis close-ups, cranes against city skylines, anything that reads as housing. The site's own photography is soft English light and greens; the stock has to sit next to it.
