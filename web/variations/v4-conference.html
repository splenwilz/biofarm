Overview

An open discussion on the realities of BNG in practice

Biodiversity Net Gain is attracting growing interest from farmers and landowners. But where does it fit within a working farm business? Join Biofarm and a panel of leading voices from farming, policy, nature recovery and land management.

Speakers

Hosted by Chris Rowland-Smith, Land Manager at Biofarm. Across the two days, panel members include:
