Biofarm at Groundswell 2026

BNG on a Working Farm

Biodiversity Net Gain is attracting growing interest from farmers and landowners. But where does it fit within a working farm business?

Reserve Your Place Meet the Panel

Two sessions · Wed 1 & Thu 2 July, 12.00–12.45pm · Pasture Field, Stand D6

On the agenda

What we’ll discuss

Biodiversity Net Gain is creating new opportunities for farmers and landowners, but it’s also raising important questions.

Where does BNG fit within a productive farm or estate? How does it compare with other diversification opportunities? What are the long-term commitments? And how do you balance commercial returns with the practical realities of managing land for nature?

Join Biofarm for an honest, practical discussion exploring the opportunities, challenges and long-term realities of BNG on working land.

Bringing together leading voices from farming, landownership, policy, nature recovery and habitat management, the panel will share different perspectives, real-world experience and practical insights to help landowners make informed decisions about whether BNG is the right fit for their land and business.

Whether you’re already exploring BNG or simply want to understand where it sits within the future of rural land management, you’ll leave with a clearer understanding of the opportunities, the risks and the questions every landowner should be asking.

Leading voices across farming, land & nature recovery
